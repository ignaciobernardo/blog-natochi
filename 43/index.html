Growth levers for startups, ventures and cool things.

I'm Ernesto Bernardo, natochi for short: a dropout based in Santiago de Chile. Over the last 6 years I've built businesses, communities and fun stuff at the places I've worked.

Right now I'm a generalist at Platanus, contracting for Kairos and the M3 Fellowship, and helping run Gen Stream. On the side I think about AI safety, cognitive science, venture capital, community stuff, and anything Borges related.

Eighteen projects across communities, hackathons, funding, tools and AI safety, each paired with one of my hand-drawn stamps, all on a single sheet. Hover or tap a stamp to read the back of it, or pick an area and the rest of the sheet fades out.

The loop

Most of it is one loop, run over and over. Meet people, gather them into communities and meetups, put them through 36-hour hackathons, ship projects and startups, then back the ones that come out with grants and angel checks. Whoever goes around once comes back as a friend, and friends bring the next people in.

Writing

Posts when something is worth saying, and a newsletter, natochi updates, when enough has happened. Lately I'm reading In Search of Lost Time (Marcel Proust) and watching Sōsō no Frieren (Kanehito Yamada).
