Help

Answers, and how to reach the team

Prototype: people and projects are samples, and everything you do is saved only in this browser.

Questions

  • In this prototype, nobody else. Everything is saved in your browser only, so another device or browser starts fresh. The real platform will share your project, standups and bookings with the cohort and the team.

  • Use “Reset sample data” in the banner at the top. It clears everything you've entered on this device and brings back the sample cohort.

  • Every time is in Pacific time first, because week one is in the Bay Area. If your device is set to another time zone, your local time is shown beside it.

  • Cancel it on Office hours as early as you can, so someone else can book the slot.

  • Links are set by the team before week one. Until then the Join buttons stay off. If a link is set and fails, write to the program team.

  • Log your hours as you go and export them from Hours and pay. The payout process itself (invoices, schedule, method) is set by the team in the pay document.

  • The code of conduct will set out how concerns are handled. Until it's published, write to fernando@m3fellowship.com. You don't need to have signed the code to raise a concern.
