Cohort 02 · How it runs

One week in person, a paid month on your own project, a demo day, and two more months for the projects that are working.

Bay Area · San Francisco or Berkeley. Dates marked To confirm are planned but not final yet.

The deal

Rate
Your rate, up to $100 an hour
Hours
About 10 hours a week, during the build
Week one
In person, Jan 23–Jan 31
Direction
Yours. Nobody directs your work.

Phases

Dates in Pacific time
  1. 01 · Now

    Week one

    Jan 23 – Jan 31, 2027
    In person · Bay AreaTo confirm

    In person. Mornings are research blocks: you read the field and write down what is missing. Afternoons are discussion sprints, with field experts there to red-team the parts you cannot see.

  2. 02 · Now

    Build

    Feb 1 – Feb 28
    RemoteTo confirm

    A paid month on your own project, alongside your job, at your rate up to $100 an hour. A weekly standup with three peers and one facilitator keeps the accountability tight. Nobody directs your work.

  3. 03 · Now

    Demo day

    Mar 5, 2027
    Venue to confirmTo confirm

    You present what you built to the cohort and to the people who can move it forward. Papers get routed to workshops and arXiv referrals, tools go to the open source community, and new orgs get introductions to funders.

  4. 04 · Now

    Extension

    Until May 31
    By invitationTo confirm

    Promising projects get two more financed months to keep going at the same rate and cadence, with the same routing onward when the work is ready.

How pay works

  1. Step 01Set your hourly rate, up to $100
  2. Step 02Log the hours you work on your project
  3. Step 03Export your log for an invoice
  4. Step 04The team pays out, on a schedule and by a method they set
For scale, not a promise10 hours a week for four weeks at the $100 cap is $4,000. The real payout process is set by the team in the pay document.
