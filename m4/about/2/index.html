  1. LØG-01OriginLogged

    M3 is formed

    A collaboration between three organisations, each already working on AI safety or security.

    • P-01ConstellationAn independent research center for people tackling the hardest problems in AI safety and security.
    • P-02KairosA nonprofit building talent infrastructure for AI safety, from research mentorship to the programs that produce the field's operators and founders.
    • P-03AISBA 7-day intensive program for security professionals shaping how we secure emerging AI systems.
  2. LØG-02Aug 30 – Sep 4, 2026Done

    Cohort 01 · week one, London

    7 senior security people, 8.6 years in the field on average, in London alongside AISB. None worked in AI safety before M3.

    In person. Mornings are research blocks: you read the field and write down what is missing. Afternoons are discussion sprints, with field experts there to red-team the parts you cannot see.

    They came from

    Former head of DFIR for the US Army · Apple · Microsoft · UC Berkeley · Singapore's national water agency (IT/OT) · Israel Defense Forces

  5. LØG-05Week 6 · Oct 3Program

    Demo day

    You present what you built to the cohort and to the people who can move it forward. Papers get routed to workshops and arXiv referrals, tools go to the open source community, and new orgs get introductions to funders.

  6. LØG-06Months 2–3Program

    Extension, by invitation

    Promising projects get two more financed months to keep going at the same rate and cadence, with the same routing onward when the work is ready.

  7. LØG-07After cohort 01Logged

    What changes

    Δ01 · Longer selection

    Outreach starts two to three months ahead, and every candidate does at least two interviews and a take-home.

    Δ02 · Time to say yes

    A much longer window to accept, get employer authorization and sort visas.

    Δ03 · Pods

    Twenty fellows split into small groups that work and meet together, so the peer dynamic that worked at seven holds.

    Δ04 · Employer track

    A pilot of accelerated interviews and project ideas hiring orgs accept as work trials. M3 stays independent, not a hiring pipeline.
