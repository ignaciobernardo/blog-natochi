About M3

A salary-matched fellowship for senior security professionals.

A week in London to scope your project, then up to three paid months to work on it. A collaboration between Constellation, Kairos and AISB.

    Contracting for Kairos to bring cyber talent into the field. Recruited about 14 judges in three days for an AI incident-response sprint.

    “Airgap the AI”: a working OT simulation on local models, now being deployed, and a critical-infrastructure research org that grew out of it.

  4. F04Founding

    Ex-Apple security engineer. Pivoted an AI security startup toward catastrophic-risk work and is applying for nonprofit funding instead of raising VC.

  5. F05Technical research

    Adaptive scheming over long trajectories: nine configurations, three monitor types by three levels of agent awareness.

  6. F06Technical research

    Whether agents defer to human authorization based on how competent the principal seems, across 3B, 7B and 70B open models.

  7. F07Policy

    An explainer on catastrophic risk from commercially driven cyber AI, circulated to policy groups, plus an offensive-cyber practitioner workshop.
