Planning Temporal Manifolds

  • Ian Rios-SialerIndependent
  • Shantanu DarveshiIndependent
  • Justin ShenkIndependent AI Safety Researcher

Temporal Representational Geometry: We test whether the time horizon a language model is planning over can be read directly from its internal activations. Building on our finding that time horizons form a geometric manifold in single decisions, we extend it to multi-step planning, as a first step toward monitoring and steering how far ahead AI agents plan.

Auditing Compute Thresholds Across Frontier Training Runs

  • Janika SchmittSentinel Bio

Compute thresholds are the lever most current AI regulation reaches for, but the reported numbers behind them are inconsistent and rarely audited. This project builds a reproducible estimate for recent frontier runs and asks what a threshold can and cannot actually catch.

Do Steering Vectors Survive a Change of Scale?

  • Katja GraceAI Impacts

Steering vectors give fine-grained control over a model without retraining it, but almost every published vector is fitted to one model at one size. This project tests whether the same directions hold as a family scales, and what breaks first when they do not.

Screening Gaps in Benchtop DNA Synthesis

  • Yiyou SunUC Berkeley

Benchtop synthesisers move sequence screening from a handful of centralised providers to thousands of individual devices. This project maps where current screening protocols fail on that hardware, and what a practical audit of the gap would need to measure.
