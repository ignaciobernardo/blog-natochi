Sandbagging detection under distribution shift

  • Tomás Herrera
  • Mentored by Dawn Song

Abstract

A model that underperforms deliberately on an evaluation is hard to distinguish from one that is simply weak on that distribution. We build a benchmark of paired capability and evaluation tasks that differ only in framing, and test whether existing sandbagging detectors survive a shift in task domain. Detectors calibrated on one domain lose most of their discriminative power on another, suggesting current results overstate how ready these methods are for deployment.
