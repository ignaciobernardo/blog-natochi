# NASA Black Marble, 2016

The bundled `earth-night-4k.webp` and `earth-night-8k.webp` are resized WebP
versions of NASA's 2016 grayscale 3 km night-light map (13500 × 6750).

The `europe-night-4k.webp` and `europe-night-8k.webp` files combine a crop from
the 500 m grayscale tiles B1 and C1 (21600 × 21600 each), covering 15°W–40°E
and 30°N–65°N. These are used to sharpen the Europe / Horizon view; the full globe
remains geographically mapped. Regional and global images feather together.
