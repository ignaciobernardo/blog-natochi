Become a Mentor

We think Pathfinder mentorship is a strong opportunity for impact, as it allows you to guide and support the development of the next generation of university AI safety groups.

Am I a good fit?

We're looking for mentors with:

  • Previous experience organizing a university group, typically at least 1 year.
  • Strong interest in mentorship.
  • Reliability and good communication skills.

If you fit the first two criteria, we encourage you to express interest rather than worrying about whether you meet our bar for the rest.

Applications for Fall 2026 have closed. Sign up to hear about the next round.

Express Interest

Details

Commitment

  • Attend the Pathfinder kickoff call, or watch the recording if this isn't possible.
  • Be available for three weekly hour-long calls with your mentee.
  • After these initial calls, continue to meet with your mentee monthly or bimonthly according to preference.
  • Attend ~monthly 30-min check-in calls with the Kairos team.

Expectations

  • Do your best to be available at times that work for your mentee.
  • Show up prepared and on time for mentorship calls, and make sure your mentee does the same.
  • Be encouraging, supportive, and warm to your mentee.

Compensation

  • $100 USD for the first three calls
  • $300 USD for continuing through the semester

Questions? Contact us at pathfinder@kairos-project.org.
