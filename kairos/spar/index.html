At our workshops

As breakthroughs in artificial intelligence and biotechnology have rapidly progressed, experts have increasingly spoken out on the risks.

Our events are designed for participants to evaluate these arguments. Over a weekend-long workshop, participants explore the case for improving safety measures for AI and biotechnology, and hear first-hand about the career journeys of professionals working in the fields.

Ultimately, our goal is to give you the information you need to work out where you stand on these issues themselves and how you might approach careers that try to tackle them.

What is SPAR

A part-time, remote research program pairing aspiring researchers with professionals addressing risks from AI. Mentees gain research experience and guidance; mentors get capable collaborators.

Express interests

Commit 5–40 hours/week, depending on your availability. SPAR is designed to fit well alongside other commitments.

3 months of structured research, culminating in Demo Day — posters, talks, and a career fair with organizations like METR, Redwood Research, GovAI and MATS.

We accept mentees with relevant technical or policy background at any level, from undergraduate students to mid-career professionals.

Am I eligible?

The program is open to undergraduate, graduate/PhD students, and professionals of different experience levels. Mentors often look for a technical background (e.g., ML, CS, cybersecurity, math, biology, physics, etc.) or knowledge relevant to policy and governance (e.g., law, international relations, public policy, political science, economics, etc.).

Some projects may require additional background knowledge, like knowledge of specific research areas, techniques, or tools (e.g. ML frameworks).

We don't require previous research experience, and many mentees have none.

Even if you do not entirely meet these criteria, we encourage you to apply! Many past mentees have been accepted even though they didn't completely match a project's criteria.

FAQ

What is the program timeline?

Applications open roughly two months before each round begins. Matching runs for about three weeks, and the research period itself is three months, closing with Demo Day.

Does SPAR include AI policy and biosecurity project

Yes. Alongside technical projects, each round carries governance, policy and biosecurity work — the Projects page lets you filter by category.

Can I apply to multiple projects

You can. We ask that you only apply to projects you would genuinely accept, since mentors read every application.

Can I edit my application after submitting?

Until the deadline, yes — reopen the form and your answers will be there. After the deadline applications are locked for review.

How do I know my application after submitting?

You will get a confirmation by email immediately, and a decision from each mentor you applied to once matching closes.

What if I miss the application deadline?

Deadlines are firm, but rounds run twice a year. Express interest and we will write to you when the next one opens.
