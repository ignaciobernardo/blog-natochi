“How to prepare sound for your audiovisual piece.”
You will work on a sound object (ping-ing) of various
length analogue to Peter Cook’s “Archigram” idea of
a plug-in unit out of pre-cast units with a different
functions, living, transport etc.)
The object will be conceived to a sonic structure and
the “outcomes” of the workshop will be connected impro-

afterparty. At the end, each participant will get a CD-R

welcome. From amateurs to professionals. Create with

Robert Lippok:

Avant-garde German visual artist and composer Robert Lippok has been an influential player in Berlin’s thriving experimental music scene from a very early age. In 1983, he co-founded dissident punk band Ornament und Verbrechen with his brother Ronald, inspired by industrial trailblazers Throbbing Gristle. An open platform to explore jazz, electronic, and industrial concepts with likeminded contemporaries of the East German underground persuasion, the group remained active until the mid-1990s, at which point it was overtaken by the brothers’ next and most well-known collaboration, the polyrhythmic To Rococo Rot. A significant post-rock / electronic outfit started by the Lippok brethren and Düsseldorf bass guitarist Stefan Schneider, the Krautrock band ushered in a new generation of electronic acts committed to acoustic investigations and improvisation of all stripes. Known for his expansive imagination, inventive rhythmic patterns, and layers of fuzzy tones, Lippok’s solo work is just as wide-ranging — from funky, glitchy, twisted techno record Redsuperstructure for Raster-Noton (2011), to stage designs for operas, gallery exhibitions, and notable collaborations with Italian harpist Beatrice Martini, Canadian percussionist Dasha Rush or brother Ronald and Italian composer Ludovico Einaudi (for latter two in Whitetree).
