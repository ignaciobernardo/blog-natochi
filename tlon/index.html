NOW BUILDING

tlon is a small design studio of friends, and we only work on AI safety. We make the brands, sites and tools that help research labs, fellowships and nonprofits explain what they do and reach the people they need.

So far that's the Kairos site and its program sub-brands, and the M3 fellowship's landing, motion and design system. We keep the team small, move in days rather than months, and hand over code that someone else can pick up.

Whatever the project, the work stays clean, fast, and pointed at a problem that matters.
