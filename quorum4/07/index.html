Helping the world navigate AI risk.

Quorum advances research and public understanding of the safety and societal implications of AI and other emerging technologies.

Since 2023

Quorum convenes the people best-positioned to help the world navigate AI risk.

We run both invitation-only and open-application events.

  1. 2023

    Annual · Invitation only · Since 2023, in the United States and the United Kingdom

    The first Summit on Existential Security

    An invitation-only retreat for thinkers and decision-makers working to safely navigate existential risks from AI. Each Summit brings together 100–250 researchers, policymakers, funders, and leaders from AI companies and nonprofits for several days of talks, discussions, and private meetings.

  2. February 2026

    Survey report · February 2026

    Survey of AI safety leaders on AI risk, AGI timelines, and resource allocation

    This survey contains forecasts and views from attendees of the 2026 Summit on Existential Security on catastrophic risks from advanced AI, AGI timelines, and how the field should allocate its resources.
