Events/Summit on Existential Security

Summit on Existential Security

An invitation-only retreat for thinkers and decision-makers working to safely navigate existential risks from AI. Each Summit brings together 100–250 researchers, policymakers, funders, and leaders from AI companies and nonprofits for several days of talks, discussions, and private meetings.

Size
100–250 people
Format
Several days of talks, discussions, and private meetings.
Who
Researchers, policymakers, funders, and leaders from AI companies and nonprofits.
Where
United States and United Kingdom, every year since 2023.

The Summit is invitation only. To suggest someone, write to team@quorumai.org.
