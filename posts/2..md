to admire, you need greatness.

to keep going (besides talent or genius) you'll need other spiritual attributes.
- the courage to tell your truth
- the tenacity to keep going
- faith in what you have to say
- disbelief in your own strength
- modesty before the giants
- arrogance before the idiots
- affection and bravery in being alone (steering clear of little cliques and glass galleries).

in those moments the only thing that can help you is the memory of those who wrote alone.

if you're willing to suffer, to tear yourself apart, to put up with malice and pettiness, incomprehension and stupidity, resentment and infinite loneliness, then yes. you're ready to bear witness.

but to top it all off, nothing guarantees you the future.
