FAQ

Common questions

Who is eligible?
Smart, curious and ambitiously altruistic students aged 14–19 who haven’t yet started university.
How much does Leaf cost?
You choose a tier based on ability to pay, including fully free. Admissions are entirely merit-based, with no consideration of ability to pay. Leaf is a US-registered 501(c)(3) non-profit.
Does this fit around school?
Most of each week is self-paced. The fixed part is one group discussion call per week; talks, peer sessions and community events are optional.
What is Leaf’s relationship to Non-Trivial?
Non-Trivial is Leaf’s partner research program. Many alumni continue there for mentored research projects, grants and scholarships.
What is the connection between Leaf and effective altruism?
Leaf is inspired by movements like effective altruism and moral ambition, but treats no set of ideas as dogma. Students are encouraged to wrestle with hard questions and engage critically.
