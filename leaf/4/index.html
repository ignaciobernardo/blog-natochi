What five weeks at Leaf looks like

Four online courses, one cohort, about five hours a week. Here is the workload, the evidence from last cohort, and what happens after.

A 501(c)(3) non-profit · courses for ages 14–19

Leaf runs five-week online courses for smart, curious and ambitiously altruistic students who haven’t yet started university. The autumn cohort runs from 12 Oct to 22 Nov, and applications close on 1 Oct.

The four courses

Every course shares the same spine: a self-paced module, one group discussion call with tutorial break-outs, and a written or project component. What changes is the subject and how much you write.

Students in The Mathematics of Morality work through problem sheets and home labs; Power, Policy and Progress asks for weekly position papers; Dilemmas and Dangers in AI has the heaviest exploration sheet, up to 3 h a week.

What students say

“Perhaps the most impactful programme I’ve ever done. I explored how to maximise my impact.” One alumna put it more concretely: “Leaf led me to change my degree subject and consider Oxford University; I’ve now got an offer.”
